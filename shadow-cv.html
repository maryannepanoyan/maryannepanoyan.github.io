Why a Shadow CV?

The parts of academia we don't always talk about.

In academia, the conventional curriculum vitae (CV) serves as the gold standard for showcasing accomplishments, qualifications, and contributions. However, alongside this document lies another, often unseen, counterpart: the shadow CV.

A shadow CV includes the experiences and challenges that individuals encounter throughout their academic journey, but that may not find their way onto a traditional CV. These can include rejected papers, unsuccessful grant applications, failed experiments, missed opportunities, and other setbacks.

I share my shadow CV as a reflection of the complete academic journey. While traditional CVs often present an idealized version of a career, a shadow CV offers a more holistic perspective, revealing the resilience and perseverance that are often required in academic pursuit.

By sharing my setbacks, I hope to contribute to a culture of openness, destigmatize failure, and foster a more supportive academic environment. We often forget that before our greatest accomplishments, we all stumble and fall. By being transparent about the mistakes and disappointments I have experienced along the way, I hope we can all accept that "to err is human."

Reflection

Failure is part of the process.

Every unsuccessful application and rejection represents an experience that shaped how I approach research, writing, teaching, and academic work. These moments have taught me to revise, ask for feedback, try again, and recognize that one outcome does not determine the value of the work that led to it.

This is a living document, and I intend to continue adding to it as my academic journey develops. My hope is that sharing these experiences makes academic failure feel a little less isolating and reminds others that behind every successful CV is a much longer story.
