I aim to create learning environments where students feel comfortable asking questions, connecting concepts to real-world examples, and developing confidence in scientific thinking.

Teaching philosophy

Learning should feel accessible.

I believe effective teaching is more than delivering information. It involves creating opportunities for students to engage with ideas, practice skills, make mistakes, and develop their own understanding.

My approach emphasizes clarity, active learning, inclusivity, and connecting course material to the questions scientists and researchers actually investigate.
