About Mary Anne

I am a PhD Candidate in Evolutionary Anthropology at the University of Toronto, with a Collaborative Specialization in Global Health. My work is based at the Krembil Centre for Neuroinformatics at CAMH.

My research interests sit at the intersection of genetics, epidemiology, human biology, lifestyle, and brain health. I use quantitative and computational approaches to investigate complex relationships that are relevant to human health and disease.

This site shares my academic work, teaching experience, and the interests that shape me both inside and outside of research.
