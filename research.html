Research

Understanding health
through genetics.

My doctoral research combines genetic epidemiology and quantitative methods to investigate relationships between lifestyle, cardiometabolic traits, and brain health.

Research statement

My research journey

My research statement describes the development of my research interests from my undergraduate training in molecular genetics and forensic science, through my master's work in biomedical informatics, to my current doctoral research in evolutionary anthropology and global health.

It also discusses the questions that connect my projects and the future directions I hope to pursue at the intersection of genetics, human health, behaviour, and brain aging.
