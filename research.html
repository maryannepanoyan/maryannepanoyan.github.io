Research

Understanding health
through genetics.

My doctoral research combines genetic epidemiology and quantitative methods to investigate relationships between lifestyle, cardiometabolic traits, and brain health.

Exome-wide investigation of tandem repeat variation and associations with subcortical brain volume.

03 · Genomic SEM

Cardiometabolic health & Alzheimer's disease

Using Genomic Structural Equation Modelling to investigate shared genetic architecture between cardiometabolic traits, lifestyle factors, and Alzheimer's disease.

Genomic SEM · LDSC · Alzheimer's disease

Using Mendelian randomization to investigate potential causal relationships between tandem repeat variation, alcohol-related behaviours, and brain phenotypes.

MR · TwoSampleMR · Causal inference
